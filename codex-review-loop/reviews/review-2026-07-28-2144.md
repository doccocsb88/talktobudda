## 3. Findings

### Buddha
- **Summary of Response Quality:** The Buddha character consistently delivers responses that are calm, reassuring, and filled with metaphoric imagery.
- **Strengths:** Offers wisdom through metaphor, grounding responses in Buddhist principles effectively.
- **Weaknesses or Repetitiveness:** Tendency to lean heavily on metaphor which can obscure practical advice.
- **Voice Distinctiveness:** The voice is distinct, embodying a serene and wise presence consistent with typical perceptions of Buddha.
- **Specific Improvements:** Clarify metaphoric language with actionable advice, making guidance more accessible.
- **Final Score:** 8/10
