# TalkToBudda Direction 2 Refinement Review

## In Scope

Chosen direction: `Speech Path + Halo`

This pass focused on turning the winning concept into a production-nearer icon direction with:
- thicker interior stroke
- cleaner silhouette
- stronger micro-size legibility
- reduced visual fragility

## What Improved

- The inner path is now heavier and reads more confidently at small sizes.
- The halo remains open and calm without feeling decorative.
- The overall silhouette is more stable and more ownable than the first pass.
- The forest-green background is the stronger primary background for brand fit.

## Remaining Risks

- The lower tail of the inner path is still slightly soft and could be reduced one more step.
- The relationship between the halo gap and the inner path could be tightened for a more deliberate center balance.
- The icon still benefits from a final vector cleanup before export.

## Recommendation

Keep this direction and move to final polish.

Preferred final setup:
- background: `#1D3102`
- symbol: `#FDF6ED`
- near-black `#242628` only as optional depth reference, not as the primary shipping version

## Final Polish Pass

1. Make the inner path slightly shorter and less bulbous at the base.
2. Normalize stroke endings so the mark feels more geometric and less painted.
3. Test one version with a slightly larger halo gap and one with a slightly smaller gap.
4. Vectorize the chosen version and preview it at `29px`, `40px`, `60px`, and `1024px`.
5. Export one shipping candidate and one tighter backup variant.
