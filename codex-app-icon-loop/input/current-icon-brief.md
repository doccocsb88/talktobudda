# TalkToBudda App Icon Brief

## Product

`TalkToBudda` is a mobile app centered on spiritual guidance, Buddhist reflection, calming conversation, meditation support, and emotionally grounded daily use.

## Product Promise

The icon should communicate:
- calm
- wisdom
- trust
- modern guidance
- spiritual depth without feeling old-fashioned or overly religious

## Audience

Primary audience:
- users seeking calm, reflection, emotional support, and a gentle spiritual companion

Avoid making the product feel:
- preachy
- generic wellness
- childish
- cartoon religious
- visually noisy

## Visual Direction From Current App

Observed brand palette in the repo:
- deep forest green `#1D3102`
- warm near-black `#242628`
- soft ivory `#FDF6ED`

These should anchor the first icon explorations unless a stronger reason is given.

## Symbol Territory To Explore

Prefer symbols related to:
- lotus
- halo
- speech / conversation
- quiet light
- path to clarity
- abstract Buddha-inspired calm geometry

Avoid:
- literal full-face Buddha portraits
- clipart lotus flowers
- crowded mandalas
- highly detailed temple scenes
- soft-gradient wellness cliches

## Platform Constraints

The final icon must work at:
- `1024 × 1024` marketing size
- small iOS home-screen sizes where silhouette and contrast matter more than detail

The concept must survive:
- no supporting wordmark
- rounded-square crop
- small-size blur

## Desired Outcome

Produce 3 distinct icon directions:
1. safest premium direction
2. most differentiated direction
3. strongest App Store shelf-impact direction

Then recommend one direction for refinement.
