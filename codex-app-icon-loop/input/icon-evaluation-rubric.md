# App Icon Evaluation Rubric

Score each concept from `1` to `10` on each dimension.

## 1. Brand Fit

Questions:
- Does it feel aligned with calm, wisdom, trust, and spiritual depth?
- Does it match `TalkToBudda` rather than a random meditation app?

## 2. Distinctiveness

Questions:
- Would it stand apart on the App Store shelf?
- Does it avoid stock lotus / wellness cliches?

## 3. Small-Size Legibility

Questions:
- Is the silhouette still readable at iPhone home-screen size?
- Can the main shape survive blur and rounded-square cropping?

## 4. Symbol Quality

Questions:
- Is the metaphor strong, reduced, and ownable?
- Does the icon feel designed rather than illustrated?

## 5. Color Discipline

Questions:
- Are contrast and hierarchy controlled?
- Do the colors feel premium instead of muddy or generic?

## 6. Production Readiness

Questions:
- Could this direction be refined into a final `1024 x 1024` asset without a full redesign?
- Is the concept simple enough to export consistently?

## Recommendation Format

For each concept report:
- total score out of `60`
- biggest strength
- main risk
- refinement note

Winner selection should favor:
1. small-size clarity
2. brand fit
3. distinctiveness

If two directions are close, prefer the one that needs less visual cleanup before production.
