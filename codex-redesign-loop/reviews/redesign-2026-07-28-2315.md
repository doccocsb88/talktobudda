# Redesign run 2

## Scope

- Onboarding
- Quotes
- Meditation
- History
- Scriptures
- Character selection
- Chat

## What is working well

- The app now has a much clearer visual direction. The warm neutral palette and serif-led hierarchy feel intentional instead of generic.
- Onboarding is materially better than the previous pass. The image-led composition and copy card now feel like a real product intro rather than a default pager.
- Quotes, Meditation, and History now belong to the same family. Card radius, background softness, and heading treatment are mostly coherent.
- Character selection is strong. It has the clearest premium feel in the set right now, with good spacing and a confident title block.

## What is still weak or inconsistent

- `Chat` is still the weakest screen in the system. The top header is improved, but the conversation area has too much empty space, the paywall bar is visually heavy, and the input bar feels detached from the rest of the screen.
- `Scriptures` is readable but too dense. The title, search, and large stacked cards all compete at the same weight, so the screen feels long and repetitive.
- `Quotes` improved, but the hero card is too copy-heavy relative to the quote list. It steals attention from the actual content.
- `Meditation` has a stronger structure now, but the mood row still reads as five isolated stickers rather than a polished selector.
- `History` empty state is visually memorable, but the background illustration is close to overpowering the functional content.
- Tab bar quality is uneven. `Quotes`, `Meditation`, and `History` look balanced, but `Scriptures` appears washed out and less active in the captured state.

## Priority fixes

1. Refine `Chat` first.
   The bottom monetization strip and composer should be treated as one cohesive dock. Reduce visual fragmentation and tighten spacing between remaining-chats, input, and send action.

2. Reduce `Scriptures` density.
   Shorten card heights, reduce title line count pressure, and make the icon block less dominant so scanning becomes easier.

3. Simplify the `Quotes` hero.
   Keep the concept, but cut the copy or lower its visual weight so the quote feed becomes the primary content again.

4. Upgrade the `Meditation` mood selector.
   Add clearer selected-state treatment and stronger grouping so it reads like a deliberate control rather than a row of icons.

5. Tone down the `History` illustration layer.
   The empty state should keep the atmosphere, but the background should recede a bit more behind the message and CTA.
